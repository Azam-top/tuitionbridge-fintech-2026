The student's payment problem

Paying tuition means balancing four things

The amount

Tuition is billed in tenge.

The full cost

Rate and fees decide what you pay in dollars.

The deadline

Money must arrive before it, not just be sent.

Money to live on

Rent and food must stay covered.

This is our understanding of the problem. No student interviews have been run yet.

6

The AI's role

The AI explains. The server decides.

AI assistant

  • Understands your request
  • Uses the tools to read the invoice, funds and quotes
  • Explains the recommendation in plain words

Server

  • Checks every amount
  • Applies the deadline, funds and reserve
  • Ranks the options
  • Accepts approval only from you

The AI cannot approve a payment or change an amount.

7

Approval and payment safety

Approval is tied to the exact plan

  • Exact figures. Your approval covers these amounts. If anything changes, you approve again.
  • Fresh offers only. An expired offer cannot be approved.
  • Server checks. The server re-checks everything. You finish the payment at the provider.
